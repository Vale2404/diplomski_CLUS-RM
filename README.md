Ovaj kod je izmijenjena verzija CLUS-RM algoritma s repozitorija https://github.com/matmih/CLUS-RM-library i proširenje implementacija opisanih i implementiranih u diplomskom radu "Algoritamske i implementacijske optimizacije pristupa za traženje redeskripcija CLUS-RM" dostupnom na poveznici https://dabar.srce.hr/islandora/object/pmf%3A10483. Nastao je u svhu obrane diplomskog rada pod naslovom "Algoritamske i implementacijske optimizacije pomoćne šume stabala CLUS-RM algoritma" na Prirodoslovno-matematičkom fakultetu, Matematičkom odsjeku, Sveučilišta u Zagrebu, 2025. Mentor diplomskog rada je doc. dr. sc. Matej Mihelčić.

This code is a modified version of the CLUS-RM algorithm from the repository https://github.com/matmih/CLUS-RM-library, and an extension of the implementations described and developed in the master's thesis "Algorithmic and Implementation Optimizations of the CLUS-RM Redescription Mining Approach", available at https://dabar.srce.hr/islandora/object/pmf%3A10483. It was created for the purpose of defending the master's thesis titled "Algorithmic and Implementation Optimizations of the CLUS-RM Supplementing Forest" at the Faculty of Science, Department of Mathematics, University of Zagreb, in 2025. The thesis advisor is Assistant Professor Matej Mihelčić, Ph.D.
